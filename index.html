📱 How to Install

AltStore Classic • SideStore • Direct Sideloading (Recommended)

  1. Direct IPA Sideload (Easiest & Most Reliable):

    Tap the Download IPA button above. Open AltStore Classic, go to My Apps, tap the + icon, and select c64ermu.ipa.

  2. Add as a Source in AltStore Classic:
    https://markoer73.github.io/c64ermu-altstore/source.json

    Open AltStore Classic, go to the Sources tab, tap +, paste the URL, and install.

  3. ⚠️ Note for EU users with AltStore PAL installed:

    Because AltStore PAL registers the altstore:// URL scheme on your iPhone, tapping "Add to AltStore Classic" opens AltStore PAL instead. AltStore PAL will display an error: "missing a marketplaceID". To avoid this, do not tap the deep-link; instead, tap Copy URL above and paste it into AltStore Classic, or use Download IPA.

AltStore PAL (European Union • Apple DMA Marketplace)

  1. AltStore PAL is an Apple-sanctioned Alternative Marketplace under the EU Digital Markets Act.
  2. Under Apple's strict DMA rules, AltStore PAL cannot install regular sideloaded IPAs. Every app in AltStore PAL must be submitted to Apple for Notarization as an Alternative Distribution Package (ADP) and assigned a numeric marketplaceID (Apple Item ID).
  3. Community emulators without Apple DMA notarization cannot be installed directly via AltStore PAL.
  4. How to use in the EU: You can install AltStore Classic directly from inside the AltStore PAL marketplace for free, and then use AltStore Classic to sideload c64ermu.ipa!
